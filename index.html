About the program

Built by students.
Open to everyone.

Commit to Be Fit is a free fitness program for CSUN students, faculty, staff, and members of the surrounding community.

Workouts combine functional cardiovascular and resistance training to develop strength, endurance, flexibility, balance, and heart health. Every session is designed so beginners and experienced participants can train side by side.
